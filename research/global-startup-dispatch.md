# European startup dispatch

The German, French, Italian, and Spanish Yellow ROMs were independently decoded after the Japanese origin and English build. Each preserves the English dispatch boundaries at `$01ab`, `$01af`, and `$01b2` and converges before jumping to `$1d10`.

Every official language retains its own verified ROM identity, publication-safe CFG report, RGBDS source, range hashes, and manifest. Matching code is recorded as verified equivalence rather than substituted evidence.
