# Pikachu Delay3 routines

`pikachu-jp-rev0` is the Japanese origin reference. All 9 cataloged releases (Japanese, English, German, Spanish, French, Italian) contain one `Delay3` tail-call bound to the previously verified release-specific `DelayFrames` address and one `ProtectedDelay3` wrapper that preserves BC. Reports retain identities, call relationships and hashes without ROM bytes; release status is not promoted.
