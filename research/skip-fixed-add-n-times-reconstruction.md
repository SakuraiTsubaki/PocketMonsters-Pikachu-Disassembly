# Pocket Monsters Pikachu fixed-name skip and AddNTimes

The Japanese release `pikachu-jp-rev0` is the origin reference. All 9 cataloged releases (Japanese, English, German, French, Italian, Spanish) contain exactly one adjacent pair. Japanese builds use NAME_LENGTH 6; the official non-origin localizations use 11. Both addresses, the immediate length, candidate status and pair hash are retained without publishing ROM bytes.
