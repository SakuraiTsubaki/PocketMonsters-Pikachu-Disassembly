# German, Spanish, French, and Italian entry/header evidence

Exact-hash official German, Spanish, French, and Italian candidates were analyzed separately after the Japanese, Korean-when-available, and English references. Their fixed cartridge entry target set is $01ab. Each language retains separate public analysis, RGBDS source, and a visually verifiable 384x64 header-logo PNG. All four logo PNGs match the fixed Nintendo header-logo hash, while complete-ROM identities remain distinct. Reports omit raw ROM byte extracts; manifests retain slice hashes and provenance. Candidate statuses are unchanged.
